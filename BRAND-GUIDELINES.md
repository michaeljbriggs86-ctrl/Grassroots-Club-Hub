## 1. Brand in brief

- **What PitchKind is:** a team app for grassroots football clubs. Football first, family and community focused, simple and trustworthy. `[v1.4 s3]`
- **Look:** green and white, real football photography, one product family across every platform. `[v1.4 s3]`
- **Tone:** friendly, calm, inclusive, concise and professional. No corporate jargon, no childish wording, no hype, no unnecessary technical terms. `[v1.4 s4.3]`
- **Official lock-up:** the P monogram above the PitchKind wordmark ("Pitch" in dark green, "Kind" in light green) above the tagline **ORGANISE | CONNECT | PLAY**. `[v1.4 s4.1]`

- Use the supplied files only. Do not stretch, recolour, crop through, redraw, add text inside, or regenerate them. `[v1.4 s4.1]`

- Clear space: at least half the width of the P's stem on every side of the full lock-up (about 11% of its width). `[v1.4 s4.5]`
- Full lock-up at 200 CSS px wide or more. Below that, use the mark alone. `[v1.4 s4.5]` (The 200 px minimum has not been tested.)
- Do not place the full lock-up inside a circular crop. `[v1.4 s4.5]`
- On dark surfaces use the reverse asset. Never apply CSS filters to the primary logo. `[v1.4 s4.5]`
- Primary logo goes on white, not on the canvas colour `#F3F6F4` (light green measures 2.90:1 there, 3.16:1 on white). `[v1.4 s4.5]`
- Reverse logo goes on green-950 or ink only, never on green-900 (2.53:1). Measured: 3.53:1 on green-950, 5.75:1 on ink. `[v1.4 s4.5]`
- Known limit: the artwork is a cleaned vector trace with slightly uneven straight edges at very large sizes. A crisp rebuild is PLANNED if anything above about 2000 px wide is needed. `[v1.4 s4.1]`

### 4.2 Rules

- No new dominant colour for any feature, role or platform. Status colours only where meaning requires them. Page chrome stays green and white. `[v1.4 s5]`
- Never use a hex value that is not in the table. Colours that "look close" are not allowed, including in web pages, social graphics and slides. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
- No decorative gradients or novelty effects. `[v1.4 s3 principle 6]`
- Contrast: aim for WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and interface components). Check any pairing not already measured. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]` (v1.4 requires "strong contrast" without naming a standard.)
- Logo-bearing dark surfaces use green-950 or ink, and green-900 is never used behind the reverse logo. `[v1.4 s4.5]` Green-900 stays available for other dark brand areas where v1.4 section 5 permits it. No general dark theme exists in the app, so no platform defines one. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
- Print: CMYK and Pantone equivalents are NOT defined. PLANNED. Do not convert by eye; get a printed proof first. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20, TO CONFIRM]`

## 5. Typography

- Family: `Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif`. `[v1.4 s6]` No second typeface on any platform. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
- Scale (from v1.4 section 6): screen title 28-36 px at weight 800-900; section title 20-28 px at 750-900; card title 16-20 px at 700-850; body 14-17 px at 450-600 with line-height at least 1.4; caption 12-14 px; button 14-16 px at 750-900; navigation label 11-13 px at 700-850. Use `clamp()` rather than fixed oversized text.
- Body text never below 14 px. Avoid all caps for body text. `[v1.4 s14]` The tagline is all caps only inside the official lock-up.
- On the web, self-host Inter (open licence) rather than loading it from Google Fonts. Loading fonts from a third party sends every visitor's address to that company, which is a privacy risk this product should not take. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]` `[Likely]` This is a privacy-law risk in some EU rulings; not legal advice, and it still needs checking against the actual legal and business setup.

## 6. Voice and copy

- Tone as in section 1. `[v1.4 s4.3]`
- British English. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]` (Current guidelines already use it.)
- Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels. Plain verbs. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
- Use hyphens or colons in place of em dashes in brand copy. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
- Tagline: **ORGANISE | CONNECT | PLAY** appears only inside the official lock-up. Other copy may say other things but is never presented as the tagline or as part of the logo. `[v1.4 s4.1]`
- Claims must match status. Describe a feature as available only when it is DEPLOYED / LIVE, checked directly and dated. Describe a BUILT feature that is not LIVE as beta or coming soon, and say so. Describe PLANNED features as "coming soon" or leave them out. Never claim compliance, safety, approval or affiliation ("GDPR compliant", "FA approved", "safe for children") unless Mike has documented the basis. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]` (Extends protocol section 1 to public claims. The Privacy Policy and DPA are working copies, not final, as of 2026-09-19 `[ARCH s11]`.)

## 7. Imagery rules

Rules 7.1 to 7.3 come from the app guidelines and cover what the app shows. Applying them to every other platform is an extension of the app's scope. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`

### 7.1 Required `[v1.4 s7.1]`

Realistic generated or licensed photographic imagery of real-looking footballs, goals and nets, pitches and grass, grassroots players and teams, corner flags, and football environments and club activity. Natural lighting, plausible perspective, photographic depth.

### 7.2 Forbidden `[v1.4 s7.2]`

- CSS circles or polygons combined to imitate a football.
- Lines or rectangles combined to imitate a goal or pitch (this includes CSS-drawn or otherwise artificial pitch stripes and centre-circle line art; genuine photographs of mown pitches are fine).
- SVG drawings pretending to be photographic football objects.
- Cartoon footballs, clip-art football scenes, abstract blobs used as football imagery.
- Stretched, pixelated or visibly low-resolution images.

SVG is allowed for interface icons and official branding marks only. The football inside the PitchKind mark is permitted.

### 7.3 Treatment `[v1.4 s7.3]`

- `object-fit: cover` or `background-size: cover` for heroes; preserve the focal subject with `object-position`; never stretch.
- Subtle green or black overlays only where text contrast needs them.
- Hero source images at least 1600 px wide; 1920 px or wider preferred.
- No visible blockiness, blur or ringing at normal viewing size.
- One strong hero image per page or screen. No second decorative photo strip.

### 7.4 Extra rules for public platforms `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`

- PitchKind's own public channels (website, social media, store listings, email) use no image of a real, identifiable child. Use generated or licensed imagery. This is a PitchKind brand and safeguarding policy, kept separate from any legal basis a club has for its own use of photographs. Whether to allow consent-based exceptions is a decision for Mike.
- Generated people must not resemble any real, identifiable person. Prefer wider shots, back views or partial framing over close-up faces, especially for children.
- No real club kits, real league badges, sponsor logos or third-party marks inside generated images.
- Text is never baked into an image (see 11.2). Words are added in the layout, where they can be edited and checked.

## 8. Layout and components (web and other digital)

Snapshot of v1.4 sections 8 to 11 and 14, applied to the web.

### 8.1 Device classes `[v1.4 s8.2]`

Small phones 320-374 px: single column, full-width primary actions. Standard phones 375-439 px: single column, standard spacing. Large phones 440-599 px: single column or selective two-column cards. Small tablets and foldables 600-899 px: wider frame, two-column grids allowed. Large tablets and web 900+ px: centred maximum-width content, multi-column cards, forms keep readable line lengths. Portrait and landscape both remain usable.

### 8.2 General rules

- Flexible layouts; no fixed pixel positioning, no fixed content heights. `[v1.4 s8.1]`
- No horizontal scroll on standard pages. Wide tables scroll inside their own container. `[v1.4 s8.1]`
- Long pages scroll; do not shrink text or controls to fit one screen. Buttons stay at least 44 px, body text at least 14 px, fields at least 46 px. `[v1.4 s8.4]`
- Respect device safe areas and apply any top or bottom inset once, not stacked across layers. `[v1.4 s8.1.1]` (On the web, use the safe-area environment values.)
- Spacing in 4 px steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32. Screen side padding 16-22 px on phones; card padding 14-18 px; major section gap 16-24 px; control gap 8-12 px. `[v1.4 s9]`
- Radii: cards 16-20 px, fields 10-12 px, primary buttons 10-14 px. No one-off radii or shadows. `[v1.4 s9]`
- Body measure of 60 to 75 characters. `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`

### 8.3 Components `[v1.4 s10]`

- **Primary button:** dark or medium brand green, white text, strong weight, at least 48 px high, full width on phone when it is the page's main action, subtle shadow only.
- **Secondary button:** white background, green outline and text, same height and radius.
- **Fields:** white surface, `#D8E0DA` border, 10-12 px radius, at least 48 px high, clear label, focus ring in brand green, no cramped inline fields on small phones.
- **Cards:** white or soft-green, thin neutral border, 16-20 px radius, low-contrast shadow.
- **Banners:** pale green for positive or informational, pale red for errors, clear icon plus text.
- **Navigation:** green active item, muted neutral inactive, one icon family, readable labels. Use a "More" destination rather than shrinking labels.

### 8.4 Web-only additions `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`

- Visible focus state on every interactive element, in brand green.
- Motion only in response to a person's action. No entrance animations. Respect the reduced-motion setting.
- Light theme only.
- Semantic HTML, a labelled `main` region, alt text on images, meaningful link text, `lang="en-GB"`.

## 11. Image generation framework (for ChatGPT)

### 11.1 Roles `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`

- **ChatGPT generates all raster imagery.** Heroes, banners, social and store graphics, photographic scenes.
- **Claude does not generate images.** Claude checks submitted images against 11.6, checks the provenance record, and writes the log entry.
- **Mike approves** every image before it is used anywhere public or placed into source.
- **The logo is never generated.** Only the approved SVG assets are used, and they are placed on top of a finished image in layout. Turning an approved SVG into a PNG or another size is file conversion, not generation, and must keep the geometry exactly.

This mirrors the production and verification split in `AI-COLLABORATION-PROTOCOL.md` section 9, which covers app work only. Mike approved extending it to brand imagery on 2026-09-20.

### 11.2 Non-negotiables

1. Photographic and real-looking. Natural light, plausible perspective, photographic depth. `[v1.4 s7.1]`
2. No text of any kind inside the image: no slogans, no signage words, no shirt lettering, no captions. This is how the retired "More Than A Game" accent ended up baked into hero art that must now be regenerated. `[v1.4 s4.2]` `[APPROVED 2026-09-20]`
3. No logos, badges or marks: not PitchKind's, not a real club's, not a league's, not a sponsor's.
4. No constructed shapes imitating footballs, goals or pitches. `[v1.4 s7.2]`
5. Generated people do not resemble real people. Prefer wide, back-view or partial framing. Children in kit only, no close-up faces. See 7.4.
6. No real club kit or league branding. Use plain, generic kit in colours from the palette or neutral colours.
7. Green and white harmony. Natural grass green is expected; the image must sit comfortably beside the tokens in section 4 without fighting them.
8. Nothing that could make forms or data harder to read. Reserve calm areas for overlays. `[v1.4 s7.3]`
9. No hyper-stylised, cartoon or clip-art look.

```text
EXCLUSIONS:         (paste the standard exclusion block in 11.4)
OUTPUT:             (JPEG or WebP, sRGB, filename per 11.9)
```

### 11.4 Prompt structure and standard exclusion block

Build every prompt in this order. Keep it plain and concrete.

1. **Subject and action:** what is in the frame.
2. **Setting:** grassroots pitch, real grass, believable surroundings.
3. **Light and time:** direction, softness, time of day.
4. **Camera feel:** lens and viewpoint (for example eye level, shallow depth of field). Describe it as a photograph.
5. **Composition:** where the focal subject sits, and which region is calm for overlays.
6. **Palette:** natural greens and whites; no neon, no heavy colour grading.
7. **Exclusions:** the block below, always appended.

Standard exclusion block (append to every prompt):

```text
No text, letters, numbers, words, slogans or signage anywhere in the image.
No logos, badges, crests, sponsor marks, league or club branding.
No cartoon, illustration, clip-art, CGI or 3D-render look.
No close-up faces of children. No recognisable real people.
No distorted hands, extra limbs, warped goal nets, wobbly line markings or
uneven ball seams. No watermarks, borders or frames.
```

### 11.6 Verification checklist (Claude checks each one; Mike sees the result)

1. No text or numbers anywhere in the image, including small background signage and shirts.
2. No logos, crests or third-party marks.
3. No constructed-shape look; reads as a photograph.
4. Artefacts absent: warped nets, wobbly or uneven line markings, uneven ball seams, distorted hands or limbs, duplicated or melted objects.
5. People are generic and not recognisable; no close-up child faces.
6. Focal subject survives every required crop (check at 320 px wide and at 900+ px wide).
7. Overlay zone is calm enough for text or the logo at the intended contrast.
8. Resolution meets 11.5 with no visible compression damage.
9. Colours sit with the tokens; nothing pulls a new dominant colour into the page.
10. Provenance record is complete (11.7).

Any "no" means the image is not accepted.

### 11.7 Provenance record (submitted with every image)

- Brief ID and surface.
- The tool used and the date generated.
- The generation instructions as actually given, including the exclusion block, and any settings the tool really exposes (model name or version, seed and similar). Where the tool does not expose a field, write "not exposed". Do not invent metadata.
- Output file name and version.
- Confirmation that the tool's terms permit commercial use of the output. `[Likely]` Terms differ by tool and change; Mike should confirm, not assume.
- Any edits after generation (crop, resize, colour, retouch), and who made them.
- Verification result and date, and Mike's approval date.

### 11.8 Handover procedure

Required end state before Claude verifies an image: (a) the brief exists in Drive; (b) the generated files are in `98 - UPLOAD INBOX`; (c) a new inbox log file exists naming the files, the brief and the provenance record. These can happen in separate turns or with separate tools. They do not have to be one operation.

1. Mike or Claude writes the brief (11.3) and puts it in the Drive folder for the surface. Status: PLANNED.
2. ChatGPT generates, attaching the prompt and settings. Files go to `98 - UPLOAD INBOX` per protocol section 8.
3. ChatGPT creates an inbox log file in `97 - LOG INBOX` naming the files, the brief and the provenance record, per protocol section 10.2. Status: SUBMITTED.
4. Claude runs 11.6 and adds a reply log file with the result. Status: VERIFIED or REJECTED with reasons.
5. Mike approves or rejects. Approval is recorded by a new inbox log entry. Status: APPROVED.
6. Only after approval may the image be used publicly or moved into source. A rejected image is replaced by a new version, never edited in place.

The four asset states above (PLANNED, SUBMITTED, VERIFIED, APPROVED) are for image work. They are not the protocol's status words and never stand in for BUILT or LIVE on app features.
