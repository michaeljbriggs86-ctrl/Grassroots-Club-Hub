Rules & procedures

Rules & procedures⌄

Team settings below are for Shooters Hill AFC. Selkent's official match card and fixture updates are completed on the league system.

Fixture details · handbook p. 74The Club Fixture Secretary enters home details or acknowledges away details on Selkent's website by the Tuesday before the fixture. The home club gives officials and opposition ground access, kick-off and kit colours at least five clear days before the match; the away club confirms receipt and its kit colours.
Before kick-off · handbook pp. 14–16, 76–77Check physical player registration cards and complete Selkent's official match-card procedure. Ensure players' shirts have distinct numbers; confirm named substitutes with the referee where the youth-football rule applies. Use the league's emergency paper process if the official app cannot be used; card photographs do not replace physical cards.
Playing time · handbook pp. 45, 47For U8–U11 mini-soccer, aim for equal playing time where possible. Selkent recommends at least 50% of each match for every player as best practice.
After the match · handbook pp. 17, 20, 78–79Complete the official card, including score, kick-off and end times, referee name and mark, then submit it to Selkent immediately (the handbook allows a 30-minute grace period). For an emergency paper card, tell your Club Fixture Secretary the result for entry on the league website by 18:00 on matchday; do not copy the paper card into Selkent's app later. Postponed, abandoned and unfulfilled matches need separate reporting through the league website by 18:00. Shooters Hill AFC's Match played is only your club's internal record.

The handbook gives conflicting receipt deadlines for emergency paper cards; check with your Club Fixture Secretary before relying on one. U9 and U11 registration limits also conflict across sections.

Selkent staff steps

League tasks are completed on Selkent's system. Shooters Hill AFC's fixture details and Match played are club records.

  1. Before: Check with the Club Fixture Secretary that the official fixture details and kit notices are handled.
  2. At the ground: Check physical registration cards and distinct shirt numbers; confirm named substitutes where required. Complete the official match-card procedure before kick-off.
  3. After: Include referee name and mark, then submit the electronic card promptly through Selkent (30-minute grace period).
  4. If paper or unplayed: Tell your Club Fixture Secretary; the result or postponed/abandoned report goes on the league site by 18:00 on matchday. Do not transfer a paper card into the official app.

Which records should the club check?

Eligible under-18 coaching roles need an FA-accepted Enhanced DBS with Children’s Barred List check. The FA renewal period is three years from issue. Safeguarding Children is valid for two years; IFAiF is valid for three.

The club tracks Enhanced DBS, safeguarding and first aid for its coaches. Missing or overdue records need a club check; this profile does not make an automatic eligibility decision.

Confirm exact dates, FA acceptance and role requirements in FA Club Portal.

Record dates and club checks only. Do not upload DBS certificates, certificate numbers or criminal-record information.
